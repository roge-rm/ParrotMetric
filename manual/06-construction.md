# Construction
> Planes, axes and points to build on, and pictures to trace.

These are under **Construct**. They don't make anything you print, but they give you places to sketch and things to measure from. They show in the view and can be picked like faces and edges.

## Planes

- **Offset plane** is parallel to a plane or flat face, a distance away.
- **Angled plane** is turned about one of its axes by an angle.
- **Midplane** is halfway between two planes or faces.
- **Plane through three points** goes through three corners or points you pick.
- **Plane through two edges** goes through two straight edges.
- **Tangent plane** touches a round face.
- **Plane along an edge** is square to an edge at a point along it, as a percentage of the way along, for sketching a sweep's shape.

## Axes

**Axis** is along a line through a point you give, along a straight edge, through the middle of a round face, or between two points. Revolve and Pattern can use it.

## Points

**Point** is at a place you type, at a corner or the centre of a round edge, or where three planes meet. Project brings points into a sketch.

## Canvas

**Canvas** lays a picture on a plane to trace over in a sketch. Set its **Width**, where its centre is and how far it's **Turned**. It isn't part of the model and doesn't export.
