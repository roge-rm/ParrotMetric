# Surfaces
> Thin sheets with no thickness, and making solids from them.

A surface is a face on its own, not closed into a solid. They're useful for shapes that are easier to make face by face. These tools are under **Create**.

- **Extrude** and **Revolve** with **As a surface** push or turn a sketch's lines into a surface, instead of filling areas. A revolve of just open lines is always a surface.
- **Offset surface** copies the faces you tap and moves the copy out by the **Distance**, or in when it's below 0, as a new surface. It's handy for a skin over a curved part.
- **Patch** makes a surface from **Sketch areas**, or with **Fill edges** fills a gap inside a loop of edges you pick, with a smooth face that meets them.
- **Stitch** joins surfaces you tap into one. If they close up all the way round, it makes a solid.
- **Thicken** gives a surface a thickness to make it a solid, to one side or **Both sides**.

**Split** trims a surface with a plane or a body, the same as it splits a solid.
