# Settings
> The layout, how much detail the view shows, and the projects folder.

Settings is on the start screen and in the menu under the parrot.

- **Layout** is **Automatic**, picked by the window's size, or always **Phone** or always **Large screen**.
- **Display detail** is how finely curved faces are drawn. **Automatic** picks it from a quick speed test the first time the app runs, and shows what it picked. Lower detail keeps the view smooth on older devices. It doesn't change what you export.
- **Projects folder** is a folder or a WebDAV server to keep your designs in. See Files.
