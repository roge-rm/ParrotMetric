# Checking a part
> Measuring, sections, and checks for printing.

These are under **Inspect**.

## Measure

Tap faces and edges to see their sizes, and the distance and angle between two of them. It also shows the body they're on: its volume and overall size, its area, its centre of mass, and its weight if it were solid in the material you choose: PLA, PETG, ABS, ASA, TPU, nylon, resin, aluminium, steel or brass. A print with less infill weighs less.

## Section

Cuts the view through on a plane, to see inside. Pick the plane and slide it through the part. **Keep the other side** shows the other half. It only changes the view, not the part.

## Print check

- **Overhangs** colours the faces that lean out further than **Steepest**, which need support when printed.
- **Thin walls** colours the parts of walls thinner than **Thinnest**, which may not print.

## Surface check

- **Zebra** lays stripes over the part, as many as **Stripes**. Where two faces meet smoothly the stripes run on without a break.
- **Curvature** colours how tightly each part of a face curves, against the **Radius** you set.
- **Draft** shows whether a part comes out of a mould, pulled out square to **Top**, **Front**, **Right** or a **Picked face**, or **The other way**. Faces sloping out by at least the **Least draft** show green, or blue on the side it's pulled away from. Red faces slope less than that and would drag. Grey faces lie across the pull and come straight off.

## Interference

Lists the bodies that overlap each other and how much, which on parts meant to fit together usually means something's wrong.
